## Addendum: how the in-site distances were produced (2026-11-22)
- Venue coordinates: from the Apple Maps place link supplied by the couple (33.2983987, -117.1461074).
- Hotels/airports: geocoded from street address via OpenStreetMap Nominatim (`scripts/build-geo.mjs`). The Welk and SpringHill pins resolve to the street address area (golf course / house point), so pins are approximate.
- Distances and times: OSRM (OpenStreetMap road data), typical conditions, no traffic. Results in `src/generated/geo.json`: hotel to venue 6.2 mi/11 min (Welk), 12.2/16 (Best Western), 14.6/19 (SpringHill), 15.4/24 (Residence Inn); SAN to venue 44.4 mi/57 min.
- These are labeled approximate on the site. They are not Google/Apple figures; guests are pointed to the live Maps links.
- Hotel amenities on the site are limited to what search results attributed to each official page; brand pages blocked automated fetches. Parking fees were deliberately not published.
- No hotel photographs were collected (rights not established).
